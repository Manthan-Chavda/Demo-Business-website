Explore Boulia

Capital of the Channel Country

Boulia may have around 200 locals, but its prehistoric history, folklore, and natural beauty make it one of Australia’s most memorable outback stops.

💡

Min Min Encounter

World-acclaimed visitor experience with a 45-minute animatronic theatrical show narrating the haunting, unsolved mysteries of the supernatural Min Min Light.

🦕

Boulia Heritage Complex

Home to ancient marine reptile fossils from Queensland’s inland sea (Ichthyosaurs and Plesiosaurs), historic pioneer medical artefacts, old pastoral machinery, and a rare 1888 stone cottage with an underground cellar.

🌅

Burke River

Watch vivid golden sunrises and red-ochre sunsets over the river red gums from the bridge. A quiet haven for birdwatchers, morning strolls, and relaxing by the waterholes.

🏊‍♂️

Boulia Sports Centre

Take a cooling dip in the 25-metre public swimming pool after a long hot desert drive. Facilities include an air-conditioned fitness gym, basketball court, and squash courts.

🛒

Town Services & Practical Essentials

Boulia has you covered: fuel roadhouse with diesel, convenience store offering fresh produce and pantry staples, Australia Post agency, classic outback hotel with bistro dining, mechanical repairs, tyre replacements, and hardware.

Annual Highlights

Experience Boulia’s Iconic Events

When our outback community comes together, visitors are welcomed like family. Plan your trip around these iconic gatherings.

Third Weekend of July

Boulia Camel Races

Dubbed "The Melbourne Cup of Camel Racing", this three-day spectacle brings thousands to town for high-speed camel heats, live country music, tag-along camping, and great bush banter.

Annual Outback Showcase

Boulia Rodeo & Campdraft

Real outback stockmanship at its finest. Witness thrilling campdrafting, bucking bulls, barrel racing, and evening entertainment under the outback stars.

Annual Spring Meet

Boulia Turf Club Races

A classic country racing day with dusty dirt tracks, fashion on the field, local bookmakers, and a lively community pavilion atmosphere.

Local Sports & Social

Boulia Golf Club

Play a distinctly Australian round of 9-hole golf on sand greens with wide-open fairway vistas and friendly weekend social competitions.

☀️ Seasonal Climate Guide

Peak Touring Season (April to September)

Mild daytime temperatures (22°C to 28°C) with clear azure skies. Night temperatures drop into single digits (3°C to 8°C) with desert winds. Perfect conditions for 4WD touring and camel races.

Summer & Wet Season (October to March)

Intense desert heat with daytime temperatures frequently reaching 40°C to 45°C. Monsoonal rain troughs can cause sudden unsealed road closures. Our cabins are fully air-conditioned sanctuaries.

Conquering Australia’s "Outback Way"?

Boulia is the eastern gateway of "Australia’s Longest Shortcut" linking Laverton (WA), Alice Springs (NT), and Winton (QLD). The Donohue Highway stretch between the NT border and Boulia is unsealed and remote.
